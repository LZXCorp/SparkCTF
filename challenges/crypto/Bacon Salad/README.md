# Bacon Salad

You opened the book excitedly, thinking that perhaps its yellowed pages contained unrevealed secrets and pieces of history.
But it was blank...till you turned to page 10...to a bunch of random letters? 
You asked your grandma about it, and she said:
"My dear, it's been so long. 
I don't remember, 
But I know there was something about an animal...
Or was it food? 
Since you are my little detective, why don't you figure it out?"
