# Romanized Encryption

A strange message from a mysterious port reaches you dated 49 BC, an ancient language rearranged and sent to you. Each time you listen, the words realign. Only those who understand how to realign the letters of the empire can reveal its true message. Be quick, the signal fades faster than you think.
