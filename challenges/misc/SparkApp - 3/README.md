# SparkApp - 3

Well well well, look who's been busy collecting flags like they're going out of style! You've successfully navigated through our digital obstacle course and earned yourself another shiny flag. Chef's kiss 👨‍🍳💋

Behold! You should now find yourself face-to-face with what appears to be an innocent little search query page. Don't let its humble appearance fool you - this isn't your grandmother's Google. This mysterious search interface is just dying to help you find whatever your heart desires... well, almost.

Here's where things get spicy 🌶️: Our dear database has trust issues. Like, serious trust issues. It's been hurt before and now it's more paranoid than a cat in a room full of rocking chairs.
The database has locked itself away behind a SECRET KEY that's more protected than the recipe for Coca-Cola. Without this magical incantation, your search queries will be about as useful as a chocolate teapot.
