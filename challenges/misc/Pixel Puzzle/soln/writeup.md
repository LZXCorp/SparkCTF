Given an image of a broken QR code.

Step 1:
Use QRazyBox to analyse and fix the broken QR code. Go to "Tools" and use "Brute-force Format Info Pattern". The QR code should now be fixed.
QRazyBox: https://merri.cx/qrazybox/

Step 2:
After scanning the fixed QR code or clicking decode,"tHE flag for piXel puzzle is QR_R3C0V3RY" is returned.

Step 3:
The capitalised letters spells out HEX. This is a hint to encode the flag "QR_R3C0V3RY" with HEX. The final flag is "SPARK{51525F5233433056335259}".
HEX encoder link (example, there is many others online): https://www.convertstring.com/EncodeDecode/HexEncode
