# LostDNS

Meet John, who has been working in the networking industry for 35 years. John loves to write his secrets in his notebook, or on his laptop using notepad, which generates a txt file.

John recently created a digital treasure hunt for network enthusiasts. He planted something special on flag.fluffyblob.com for a few days, then removed it to challenge digital archaeologists.

"The real treasure hunters," John chuckled  "know that the internet never truly forgets anything. There are always devices on the network that will record your moves."

**Your mission:** Prove your OSINT skills by recovering John's hidden notes from the digital archives.

**Target:** flag.fluffyblob.com

## Summary
- **Author:** Edwin
- **Category:** OSINT
- **Learning Objective:** Learn to use historical DNS services to recover deleted DNS records and practice digital forensics techniques on publicly available archives.

## Hints
- `Sometimes the most valuable information isn't what's currently visible, but what used to be there. Nothing is private once it leaves your system` (200 points)
