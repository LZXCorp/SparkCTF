# BobaQuest
My partner asked me to meet him at a Playmade outlet in the arcade where he always hangs out with his friends. Can you help me figure out where this outlet is located?

The flag is SPARK{the_full_name_of_the_mall}, in small caps. etc if the place is `Hougang mall` the flag format is `SPARK{hougang_mall}`. Any numbers will be in Arabic numerals format.
