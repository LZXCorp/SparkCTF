## Solution 

1. Find the instagram account https://www.instagram.com/ronald_siews/
2. Check out the 3rd post. https://www.instagram.com/p/DO3lAvJE4Wo/?utm_source=ig_web_copy_link&igsh=ejZ0OXc2eXlsbzg2 
3. There is a hidden QR code(middle left) on the last page of that post. 
4. When you scan the QR code you get a text: ';c#bX97(=qA7RK6?ZTp;G@+ru->.`'
5. Decode the Ascii85 text to get the flag
