## Solution
1) Check the first initials of the "Bakery Name" entries. They spell out "PASTRY", which hints that the answer lies in the PASTRY sheet.
2) Unhide all hidden sheets. You'll notice additional sheets appear which contain flags.
3) In the Bakery sheet, unhide Column P. This reveals that the actual flag is tied to the 25th entry.
4) Go to the PASTRY sheet and look at entry 25 - that's where the real flag is located.
